Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: September 20, 2026

What does ThankzWorld AI Detector do?

You text a photo or a link to a social media video to our number, and we reply with an automated verdict on whether the content appears to be AI-generated, along with a confidence level. No app or account is required.

How accurate is the detection?

Detection is powered by Hive, an industry AI-detection provider, combined with additional automated checks we run ourselves. Results are a best-effort estimate, not a guarantee, and should be treated as one signal among others rather than definitive proof. We have not published a specific accuracy percentage because we have not independently measured one against our own traffic.

What platforms are supported?

You can text a photo directly, or a video link from Instagram or Facebook. YouTube links are not yet supported — we're working on adding YouTube support and will announce it here when it ships.

How much does it cost?

New subscribers get a limited number of free checks. After that, additional checks are available as credit packs on our purchase page. Purchased credits do not expire.

Do you store my photos or videos?

No. Submitted photos and videos are analyzed automatically and are not stored after processing. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what we do retain (phone number and detection result logs).

How do I stop receiving messages?

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for assistance. See our Terms and Conditions for the full opt-in/opt-out policy.
