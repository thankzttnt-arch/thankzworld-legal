About ThankzWorld

Last updated: September 20, 2026

ThankzWorld AI Detector is built and operated by Todd Hankerson, a solo, independent founder. There's no app to download and no account to create — you text a photo or a social media video link to a phone number, and you get an automated reply telling you whether it looks AI-generated.

Why it exists

AI-generated photos and videos are showing up everywhere — on social media, in marketplace listings, in messages from people you don't know well — and most people don't have a fast, low-friction way to check one when something feels off. ThankzWorld was built to make that check as simple as sending a text.

How it works

Submitted content is run through an automated detection pipeline combining Hive, an industry AI-detection provider, with additional checks built in-house. You get a verdict and a confidence level back by text, usually within a minute or two. See the FAQ for details on accuracy, supported platforms, and pricing.

Where we stand on accuracy

We don't publish a specific accuracy percentage, because we haven't independently measured one against our own real-world traffic. Any number you might see elsewhere describing AI-detection providers generally isn't the same as ThankzWorld's own tested performance. Treat every result as a best-effort signal, not a guarantee — see our Terms and Conditions for the full disclaimer.
