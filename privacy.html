Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Business name: ThankzWorld

Service homepage: https://thankzttnt-arch.github.io/thankzworld-legal/

ThankzWorld ("we", "our", "the service") operates ThankzWorld AI Detector SMS, an SMS-based tool that lets users text a photo or a social media link (such as Instagram or Facebook) to receive an automated reply indicating whether the content appears to be AI-generated.

What information we collect

When you text our number, we receive your phone number and the content of your message (a photo or a link). We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or any other personal information.

How we use your information

Your phone number is used only to send you the detection result and to manage message credits, if applicable. The photo or video you send is analyzed automatically to produce a verdict and is not stored after processing. We keep a log of detection results (such as the score and whether the content was flagged) together with your phone number, for service quality and improvement purposes, but we do not store the actual photo or video content itself.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your use of the service. Typically, you will receive one reply message for each photo or link you submit.

Third-party services

We use Twilio to send and receive text messages, and Hive (or a similar AI-detection provider) to analyze submitted content. These providers may process your message content as part of delivering the service. We do not sell, share, or rent your mobile phone number to any third party for marketing or promotional purposes, under any circumstances.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply from your mobile carrier for texts sent to and received from our service.

Opting out

You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from this service. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention

We retain detection result logs (verdict, score, and phone number) for service improvement. We do not retain the actual submitted photos or videos. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.
